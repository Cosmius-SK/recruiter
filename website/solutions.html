Solutions & ROI

The business case, in numbers your CFO will check.

Recruitment cost isn’t mysterious: it’s recruiter hours, agency fees, and the revenue lost while seats sit empty. TalentFlow attacks all three — and proves it with its own audit data.

Cycle Time

30–50 days, compressed to the human core.

The human-paced parts — panels, interviews, notice periods — are irreducible. Everything else is administrative latency that agents remove. Per typical enterprise requisition:

Internal stepTypical elapsedWith TalentFlowMechanism

Net effect: 18–37 days of queue time removed per requisition, leaving cycle time ≈ the sum of human decision moments plus interviews and notice.

Return on Investment

Three value streams, one per quarter-page of CFO math.

STREAM 1

Recruiter capacity

8–15 hours of coordination per requisition (drafting, screening, scheduling, chasing, paperwork) drop to <1 hour of gate decisions. At a $40–60/hr loaded cost, that is $300–800 returned per requisition — or 3–5× more requisitions per recruiter.

STREAM 2

Vacancy cost

An empty engineering seat costs ~$500–1,000/day in lost output. Removing 18–37 days of latency returns $9k–37k of productivity per technical hire — the stream that usually dwarfs the others.

STREAM 3

Agency displacement

Faster internal cycles mean fewer searches escalated to agencies at 15–25% of first-year salary. Displacing even one $150k agency hire per quarter pays for the platform many times over.

What the platform itself costs to run

LLM spend is a rounding error against the streams above: roughly $6–9 per requisition on frontier models at 200 applicants (≈$3–4 with tiered models, ~$0 on the Gemini free tier during piloting), plus a small VM. Full transparency math on the pricing page.

By Stakeholder

What each seat at the table gets.

Head of Talent Acquisition

Recruiters stop being schedulers and become decision-shepherds. Every requisition gets the same rigorous process — rubric-scored screening, evidence-based decision packets, guaranteed candidate communication during notice periods. Pipeline state is queryable in real time, not reconstructed from inboxes.

CHRO / People Officer

Governance gets stronger with automation, not weaker: flagged profiles always get a human look, every hire decision carries its full evidence trail, and comp policy is enforced by code with exceptions explicitly approved and attributed. Candidate experience improves — responses in minutes, never silence.

CIO / Platform Engineering

Not a black-box SaaS: a typed, tested, self-hostable orchestration on your own GCP project. Adapter-based integrations to the systems you already run (HRIS, ATS, calendar, ITSM), a four-endpoint REST control plane, Postgres-backed durable state, and CI that needs no LLM keys.

Finance Partner

Compensation discipline by construction: offers physically cannot exceed approved ceilings; every exception arrives as a structured approval with the negotiation log attached; and every requisition reports its own fully-loaded processing cost from the audit trail.
