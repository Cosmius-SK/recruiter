FAQ

The questions procurement asks first.

Does the AI ever reject or hire someone on its own?

No. Agents score, rank, recommend and prepare. Selection decisions are made by named humans at typed gates, and profiles the screener flags as unconventional are guaranteed a human review. The hire decision is always the hiring manager’s.

What happens if the model provider has an outage?

Workflows are checkpointed at every step: in-flight requisitions park safely and resume when inference returns. Switching providers (Gemini ↔ Claude) is one environment variable — no state is lost.

Where does our candidate data live?

In your database, on your infrastructure. The only external calls are model inference, and those can run on cloud-resident model endpoints (e.g. Vertex AI) — or fully private models — to satisfy data-processing rules.

How does this fit our existing ATS — do we have to migrate?

No migration. TalentFlow orchestrates across your existing systems through an adapter layer; the ATS remains the system of record for candidates, the HRIS for budgets and bands.

Can we customise the gates and policies?

Yes — gates, loop caps, shortlist size, comp rules and routing are explicit configuration and code, not prompt folklore. Adding a gate is ~10 lines plus a payload contract; it ships with tests.

How do we evaluate it without committing?

Run the open-source pilot on one real requisition in your GCP project with free-tier inference, then compare the audit timeline against your last comparable hire. The data decides.
