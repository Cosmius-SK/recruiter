The Problem

Your time-to-fill isn’t interviews.
It’s everything between them.

Panel availability, the interviews themselves, a candidate’s notice period — those are irreducibly human-paced. Everything else in a 30–50 day hiring cycle is queue time: JDs waiting to be drafted, resumes waiting to be screened, calendars waiting to be reconciled, scorecards waiting to be chased, offers waiting to be approved, IT tickets waiting to be raised.

TalentFlow’s position: automate the queue, govern the judgement. Agents collapse days of administration into minutes. Humans keep — and gain better visibility over — every decision that matters.

Why It Can Be Trusted

Autonomy you can put in front of your compliance team.

Guardrails live in code, not prompts

The offer tool refuses any number above the approved ceiling. A confused or manipulated model cannot exceed comp policy — raising the ceiling requires a human approval recorded in state.

Humans decide; agents prepare

Six typed approval gates pause the workflow with everything needed to decide in one glance — and nothing proceeds without the answer. Flagged unconventional candidates are guaranteed a human look, never an auto-reject.

Durable, restart-proof execution

Every step is checkpointed. A workflow can park for three weeks of interviews, survive redeploys, and resume the second a scorecard arrives.

Trust but verify

After each agent acts, the orchestrator validates the result against the systems of record — and deterministically backfills anything missed, logging the correction.

A complete audit trail

Every tool call, agent decision and human approval lands in an append-only event log with actor attribution — your adverse-action and AI-governance story, built in.

Model-agnostic by configuration

Gemini for cost-free piloting, Claude for production quality — switched with one environment variable. Per-agent tiering when you want both.
